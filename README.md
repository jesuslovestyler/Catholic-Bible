# Catholic Bible — the free Catholic Bible app

The complete **Catholic Bible** as a free, installable web app — all **73 books**, including the deuterocanon, in the **Douay-Rheims Bible** (Challoner revision, American Edition of 1899), public domain.

**Live:** https://jesuslovestyler.github.io/Catholic-Bible/
**Home:** https://jesuslovestyler.com/

## What's inside

- **All 73 books** — 1,334 chapters, 35,811 verses — in Catholic order, with the traditional Douay-Rheims names: Tobias, Ecclesiasticus, 1–2 Machabees, Josue, 1–4 Kings, Paralipomenon, Esdras, Isaias, Jeremias, Ezechiel, Apocalypse
- **The deuterocanon in full** — Tobias, Judith, Wisdom, Ecclesiasticus (Sirach), Baruch (with the Epistle of Jeremiah as chapter 6), 1–2 Machabees, and the Greek additions to Esther (chapters 11–16, plainly marked) and Daniel (chapter 14)
- **Psalms numbered the Douay (Vulgate) way** — "The Lord ruleth me" is Psalm 22 — labeled plainly in the app
- **Chapter helps** — a big-idea line and section headings for the chapters, written for this app
- **Red-letter text** for the words of Jesus
- **Listen** — the printed Douay-Rheims text read aloud with your device voice
- **The whole Bible as an EPUB** download, plus a large-print PDF
- **Pass it on** — a QR card in the app, so the Bible passes hand to hand
- Works offline once installed; no account, no ads, nothing to buy

## The text

The Douay-Rheims Bible, in the Challoner revision's **American Edition of 1899** (published with the approval of Cardinal Gibbons), is the classic English Catholic translation from the Latin Vulgate, and it is public domain — free for anyone to copy and share. The text here is as published by eBible.org (eng-dra); the chapter helps were written for this app and are given away with it.

## The files

`index.html` is the whole app. `assets/` holds the Bible text, the chapter helps, the cross-references, the EPUB, and the large-print PDF. Verse data is plain JavaScript — take it and build.
